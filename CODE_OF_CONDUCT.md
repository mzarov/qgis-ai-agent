# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe and constructive community, regardless of
experience, identity, background or ability.

Helpful participation includes giving specific technical feedback, assuming
good intent, respecting different viewpoints, protecting private data and
accepting responsibility for mistakes. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
doxxing, sexualised conduct, sustained disruption and publishing another
person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and
may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement decisions
will be explained when doing so is safe and appropriate.

Report conduct concerns privately to `mzarov@duck.com`. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical. Do not use a public issue when a report contains
personal information or creates an immediate safety risk.
